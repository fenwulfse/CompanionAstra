COMPANION ASTRA — CHANGELOG

v0.2.0-alpha (2026-07-07) — "The Human Update"
- Astra's identity finalized: human cryo survivor of Vault 98, pre-war
  survey engineer, ten years awake. All remaining AI/machine-era dialogue
  rewritten (68 lines re-voiced).
- NEW: "Her Story" — nine-chapter progressive memoir in the talk menu.
  Chapters advance one per ask and persist in your save; epilogue lines
  after the story completes.
- NEW: Presence Pack — 18 dialogue systems: combat barks (attack, hit,
  power attack, bleedout, taunt, block, flee), full detection chatter
  (spot, search, alert, all-clear and transitions), hellos, and
  wait-impatient lines. 50 new voiced lines.
- NEW: spoken reactions to radiation damage, rad poisoning, swimming,
  lockpicking, hacking, entering power armor, and healing Astra.
- Affinity arc rewritten as a human story: trust, confession, love after
  ten years alone. Mechanics unchanged.
- Removed workbench/weapon-mod/armor-mod affinity accelerators (testing
  leftovers); affinity now progresses at the intended pace.
- Voice coverage audit: all 595 voiced lines verified to have audio.
