COMPANION ASTRA v0.2.0-alpha
============================

A fully voiced companion for Fallout 4. Astra is a pre-war survey engineer
who walked out of Vault 98 ten years before you woke up in Vault 111 — and
she's been watching your vault ever since, waiting for someone else to come
out of the ice.

REQUIREMENTS
- Fallout 4 (latest)
- Far Harbor DLC (DLCCoast.esm)
- Nuka-World DLC (DLCNukaWorld.esm)

INSTALLATION
Mod manager (recommended): install the archive as usual and enable
MQAstraALT.esp.

Manual: copy MQAstraALT.esp, Scripts\, and Sound\ into your
Fallout 4\Data folder, then add this line to
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Fallout4\Plugins.txt:
*MQAstraALT.esp

START
Best experienced from the start of a new game: Astra meets you outside
Vault 111. On an existing save she can be found there as well.

FEATURES
- Full companion: recruit, dismiss, trade, affinity, romance-track
  threshold scenes up to a max-affinity perk
- "Her Story" — a nine-chapter personal memoir that unfolds one piece at
  a time as you ask (dialogue wheel, Question slot)
- Combat, detection, and ambient chatter; hellos; reactions to swimming,
  radiation, lockpicking, hacking, power armor, and healing her
- Location-aware travel banter across the Commonwealth, Far Harbor, and
  Nuka-World
- Optional story questline: a four-faction cold war and the search for
  a way into the Institute (alternate main-quest companion perspective)

KNOWN ISSUES (alpha)
- Ambient comments share the game's standard 5-minute companion comment
  cooldown; reactions will not chain back-to-back
- Dialogue camera may occasionally show a wide angle in scenes
- Report anything else on the Nexus posts tab or GitHub issues
