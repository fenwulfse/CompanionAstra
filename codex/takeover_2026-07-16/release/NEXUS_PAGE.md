## Name
Companion Astra — Fully Voiced Vault 98 Survivor

## Short description (summary field)
A fully voiced human companion with a nine-chapter personal story, full
affinity arc, combat and ambient chatter, and an optional alternate
main-quest storyline. She walked out of the ice ten years before you did.

## Description

[b]She found Vault 111 on her maps years ago. She kept walking past it,
checking. Waiting for someone else to come out of the ice.[/b]

Astra is a pre-war survey engineer who went into Vault 98 the day the
bombs fell and woke up alone ten years ago — the only survivor of a failed
cryo experiment. She's spent the decade since mapping the Commonwealth
from its edges, learning who trades fair and who shoots first, and never
letting anyone close. Then your vault opened.

[b][size=4]Features[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]A companion with a past you earn.[/b] Ask about her story and she
tells it one chapter at a time — nine fully voiced chapters, from the day
of the bombs to the day she met you. Your save remembers where she left off.
[*][b]Full companion systems.[/b] Recruit, dismiss, trade, affinity with
14 threshold scenes, and a max-affinity perk. She reacts when you pick
locks, hack terminals, swim, soak rads, climb into power armor, or patch
her up.
[*][b]She talks like she's alive.[/b] Combat callouts, taunts, searching
chatter when enemies hide, all-clears, hellos, and location-aware banter
across the Commonwealth, Far Harbor, and Nuka-World. 595 voiced lines.
[*][b]An optional storyline.[/b] Follow her intel through a four-faction
cold war toward the Institute — an alternate companion's-eye take on the
main quest arc. Or ignore it and just travel together.
[/list]

[b][size=4]Requirements[/size][/b]
Far Harbor and Nuka-World DLC.

[b][size=4]Getting started[/size][/b]
Install with your mod manager and enable MQAstraALT.esp. Astra waits
outside Vault 111. Best on a fresh game; works on existing saves.

[b][size=4]Alpha notes[/size][/b]
This is an early public build under active development. Ambient comments
respect the game's standard companion comment cooldown (one per ~5
minutes). Bug reports welcome on the posts tab — save file details help.

[b][size=4]How it was made[/size][/b]
Built entirely programmatically with Mutagen — no Creation Kit — by a
human designer directing AI pair-programmers (Claude, Codex, Gemini).
Voices are Microsoft Edge neural TTS. The source is on GitHub
(fenwulfse/CompanionAstra).
