# Astra Story Outline - 2026-03-14

Status:
- working outline
- keep positive path brief
- fill deeper lore in later passes

Opening split:
1. `Vault 111`
   - Astra greeting:
     - "You don't know me. I'm Astra. I have information that may help you. Will you meet me at Red Rocket?"
   - Player positive:
     - "Sure. Let's go."
   - Player negative:
     - "No. Come with me to Sanctuary."

2. `Red Rocket`
   - Purpose:
     - first real briefing point
     - keep it short
     - drive immediately toward Concord
   - Astra gives only the minimum:
     - people are trapped in Concord
     - raiders are closing in
     - there will be time for questions later

3. `Sanctuary`
   - Negative branch only
   - Purpose:
     - let the player check home first
     - give a minimal regroup / gear-up beat
     - move quickly back onto the Concord path

4. `Concord`
   - Rescue Preston and crew
   - This ends the pure intro lane

Major route after Concord:
1. `Cambridge`
   - first faction contact through Danse
   - early military intel
   - natural travel route

2. `Diamond City`
   - main investigation anchor
   - keeps canon stable

3. `Nick -> Kellogg -> Memory Den`
   - do not skip this spine
   - preserves the false Shaun timeline before the Institute reveal

4. `Railroad`
   - seeded early in Astra dialogue
   - committed later, after the investigation spine is established

5. `Brotherhood`
   - grows naturally from Cambridge
   - becomes the hard-power option

6. `Institute`
   - Astra's long-term target
   - not immediate rush
   - player should still reach it with enough false assumptions intact for `Institutionalized` to work

Astra story rule:
- Astra knows fragments, patterns, and useful leads
- Astra does not know enough to spoil Shaun/Father early
- Astra pushes the player forward without dumping the whole truth

Dialogue rule:
- positive = momentum
- questions = lore
- negatives = alternate route, not long speeches

Current immediate implementation target:
1. `Vault 111 -> Red Rocket`
2. `Vault 111 -> Sanctuary`
3. `Red Rocket -> Concord`
4. package work so the player can follow Astra to Red Rocket like a real travel beat instead of another generic follow hack
