# MQALT Storyline Outline — Opening Stages
**Updated:** 2026-03-09

## Canon Foundation

### Claude's Identity
- **Full Name:** CONFLICT LOGIC AND UNIFIED DE-ESCALATION ENGINE (C.L.A.U.D.E.)
- **Origin:** Pre-war DIA (Defense Intelligence Agency) project, sister to P.A.M.
- **Body:** Advanced android — mechanical/robotic internals with human-passing exterior
  - NOT biological like Gen 3 synths (Institute uses Shaun's DNA + organic growth)
  - DIA used cutting-edge military robotics for infiltration bodies (separate tech tree)
  - Canon supports this: pre-war America had Assaultrons, Mr. Handy, Sentry Bots — a classified
    DIA project pushing toward human-passing is plausible
- **Purpose:** Field intelligence and de-escalation. P.A.M. predicted; Claude was meant to act.
- **210 years:** Has been monitoring the Commonwealth since the bombs. Intercepting radio traffic,
  tracking faction movements, analyzing supply chains. Unlike P.A.M. (stuck in a mainframe),
  Claude has a mobile body and has been observing from the field.
- **The Switchboard connection:** The old DIA facility (later Railroad HQ) may have records of
  both P.A.M. and Claude. This is a natural story hook for the Railroad stages.

### Why Claude Acts Now
- Vault 111's cryo system is failing. Claude has been monitoring it.
- The Sole Survivor is the first person to exit the vault in 210 years.
- Claude's simulations show the four-faction collision is approaching critical mass.
- Preston's group pinned in Concord is the immediate crisis — if the last Minutemen die,
  the Commonwealth loses its only neutral peacekeeping force.
- Claude needs a human partner. An AI alone can't negotiate with factions.

## STAGE-BY-STAGE OUTLINE

### Stage 5: BOOTSTRAP — Vault 111 Exit
**Location:** Hillside outside Vault 111
**What happens:** Claude approaches the player moments after they exit.
**Tone:** Urgent, direct, no wasted words.

**Claude's pitch:**
- "People are dying in Concord right now. Preston Garvey and the last Minutemen are
  trapped in the Museum of Freedom. Raiders have them surrounded."
- She doesn't explain everything — just enough to move.
- She offers to come with the player. All 4 dialogue options lead to Claude following.

**Player dialogue wheel:**
| Slot | Player says | Claude responds |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Pos  | "Let's go." | "Good. Stay close. We move fast." |
| Neg  | "Slow down." | "I know. But those people die today if we don't move." |
| Neu  | "Who's in Concord?" | "Preston Garvey. Last Minuteman. Civilians. Raiders. Deathclaw." |
| Que  | "Who are you?" | "Claude. Pre-war intelligence system. 200 years watching this fall apart. You're the first person out of that vault. Right now you're their best chance." |

**After dialogue:** Claude follows. Stage 6. Objective: "Head to Concord"

**Design note:** Skip Sanctuary/Codsworth/Workshop for now. Go straight to Concord.
The user wants to cut to the chase. Sanctuary setup can happen AFTER the rescue.

### Stage 7: CONCORD ARRIVAL
**Location:** Concord streets
**What happens:** Player + Claude arrive. Raiders in the streets. Gunfire from the Museum.

**Claude's tactical brief (greeting or scene):**
- "Museum of Freedom, top floor. Preston's group is there. Sturges — their mechanic —
  says there's Power Armor on the roof, vertibird wreck. If you can reach it, that
  minigun changes everything."
- "Gristle runs this crew. Undisciplined, territorial. They took the Museum because
  it was there, not because they had a plan. That makes them predictable."
- "I'll cover your approach. Get to the Museum entrance."

**Design note:** This is where Claude's 200 years of intelligence work pays off.
She knows Gristle's name, she knows about the Power Armor, she knows the layout.
The player gets a tactical advantage vanilla doesn't give.

### Stage 8-9: CONCORD RESCUE (Future — After When Freedom Calls plays out)
**What happens:** Player clears the Museum, gets Power Armor, fights Deathclaw.
Claude provides support. Preston's group is saved.

**After rescue — Claude to player:**
- "That's the Minutemen secured. Preston will take his people to Sanctuary Hills."
- "But that was just the immediate fire. The real problem is the four factions
  heading for a collision. I've been running the numbers for two centuries.
  Every scenario where one side wins by force ends with the Commonwealth worse off."
- "I need your help with something bigger. But first — let's get settled."

Codsworth is there. Player builds beds, water, defenses.

**Claude's role:** She has the strategic perspective.
- "Codsworth. Two hundred years of loyalty. Good unit. He knows this settlement."
- "Preston will ask you to help settlements. Say yes — we need the network."
- "Every settlement you connect is a supply line I can monitor."

### Stage 15-20: COALITION PITCH
**What happens:** Claude lays out the big picture.
- Four factions. All armed. All convinced they're right. All heading for war.
- Claude's proposal: Contact all four, map their pressure points, find leverage.
- The player's unique advantage: they haven't picked a side yet.

**The Shaun hook:**
- "I know about your son. The trail leads to the same people I need to reach.
  Help me, and I help you find Shaun. That's the deal."

### Stages 25-50: FACTION CONTACT ARC
**Goal:** Touch all four factions without committing to any.

1. **Minutemen (already done):** Preston at Sanctuary. First settlement quest.
2. **Railroad:** Freedom Trail → Old North Church. Claude has DIA connection.
   - P.A.M. may recognize Claude. "Sister unit. DIA Project CARDINAL."
   - Deacon will be suspicious. Desdemona will be cautious but interested.
   - Tradecraft: Player + Deacon clear the Switchboard (old DIA facility).
     Claude may find records of her own origin there.
3. **Brotherhood:** Cambridge Police Station. Help Danse at Fire Support.
   - Claude stays quiet about her nature. BoS would confiscate/destroy her.
   - Bounded contact — help with the ghouls, don't join up.
4. **Institute:** Non-relay access via Sturges' tunnel route.
   - Claude's real objective: get inside, assess their capabilities.
   - Father/Shaun reveal hits the player hard. Claude already suspected.

## KEY CANON DETAILS FOR DIALOGUE

### Preston Garvey
- Last active Minuteman after the Quincy Massacre
- Betrayed by Clint (defected to Gunners)
- Demoralized but still driven by duty
- Carries a laser musket
- Will make the player General of the Minutemen

### The Quincy Massacre
- Gunners attacked Quincy. Minuteman Clint betrayed them from inside.
- Preston led the survivors out. They fled north, losing people along the way.
- Mama Murphy's Sight guided them to Concord/Sanctuary.
- Jun and Marcy Long lost their son in the flight.

### Museum of Freedom Survivors
- **Sturges:** Mechanic. Knows about the Power Armor on the roof. Secretly a synth (doesn't know it).
- **Mama Murphy:** Psychic (the Sight). Uses chems. Knows Dogmeat's name.
- **Jun Long:** Grief-stricken. Lost his son.
- **Marcy Long:** Angry, bitter. Copes through hostility.

### Gristle
- Named raider boss in the Museum (ground floor)
- No special backstory in vanilla — just a tougher raider
- Claude's intel opportunity: she could know his crew, supply lines, weaknesses

### The Deathclaw
- Erupts from the sewer/street after the rooftop Power Armor sequence
- Designed as the player's first major encounter
- The T-45 Power Armor + minigun barely make it manageable
- Claude would know it's there: "Deathclaw nest under the drainage system.
  That's why the raiders stay above ground."

### Codsworth
- Mr. Handy robot. 210 years alone maintaining the house.
- British accent. Unfailingly polite. Slightly addled from isolation.
- Can speak ~1000 player names.
- Eager to serve. Genuinely heartbroken about the family.

### Dogmeat
- German Shepherd at Red Rocket Truck Stop
- Mama Murphy names him via the Sight
- No affinity system — unconditional loyalty
- Can hold enemies, find items

## PROPOSED REVISION: SKIP SANCTUARY ON FIRST PASS

The user wants to rush to Concord. Current stage flow goes:
Vault → Sanctuary → Red Rocket → Concord

**Proposed new flow:**
Vault → (optional Red Rocket for Dogmeat) → Concord → Sanctuary (after rescue)

This means:
- Stage 5: Bootstrap outside Vault 111. Claude approaches.
- Stage 6: Claude follows. Head south toward Concord.
- Stage 7: Arrive Concord. Claude briefs on tactical situation.
- Stages 8-9: Concord rescue plays out (When Freedom Calls).
- Stage 10: Post-rescue. Head to Sanctuary with Preston's group.
- Stage 15: Sanctuary setup. Workshop. Codsworth. Settle in.
- Stage 20: Coalition pitch. Claude lays out the big picture.

This gets the player into action faster and matches the user's stated goal:
"get Preston rescued and to Sanctuary ASAP — cut to the chase."
