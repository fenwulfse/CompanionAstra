# MQALT Storyline Direction Notes
**Date:** 2026-03-09

## Core Premise
Claude (CONFLICT LOGIC AND UNIFIED DE-ESCALATION ENGINE) is a pre-war DIA AI,
sister project to P.A.M. She's been running simulations for 200 years and has
concluded the Commonwealth's four-faction collision leads to mutual destruction.

## Key Storyline Goals (User Direction)
1. Contact all four factions (Minutemen, Railroad, Brotherhood, Institute)
2. Get Preston rescued and to Sanctuary ASAP - cut to the chase
3. Don't break canon / lore continuity
4. Claude already knows about the situation (she's been monitoring for 200 years)

## Concord Rescue Plan
- Claude knows Preston's group is pinned down at Museum of Freedom
- She brings this intel to the player as leverage at first meeting
- Multi-follower assault: Player + Claude + Codsworth + Dogmeat
  - Codsworth: already at Sanctuary, easy story tie-in
  - Dogmeat: joins at Red Rocket (natural route)
  - Claude: has the tactical plan
- Get Preston out efficiently, establish Sanctuary, pivot to real work

## Gristle (Named Raider, Museum of Freedom)
- Vanilla: just a placed named NPC, no quest role, no dialogue, no backstory
- Opportunity: Claude could have intel on him (former Minuteman? desperate supply?)
- Possible non-lethal angle (our creative space, not vanilla-supported)
- If too ambitious, skip - the rescue works fine without special Gristle content

## Multi-Follower Precedent (Canon-Safe)
- Deacon follows during Tradecraft while companion stays
- Danse follows during Fire Support/Call to Arms
- Dogmeat + Lone Wanderer perk (remnant of original dual-companion design)
- Codsworth is available immediately

## Character Identity
- Gender: Female
- Voice: en-US-AvaNeural (Microsoft Edge neural TTS)
- Race: Human (pre-war DIA infiltration prototype body)
  - Precursor to Gen 3 synths, not a synth herself
  - DIA was building human-passing bodies for covert ops before the bombs
  - Looks human but subtly "too precise"
- Age: 200+ years (canon-safe: P.A.M. and Codsworth are same era)

## Faction Dynamics
- Institute: wants to study her (proof their tech has pre-war roots)
- Railroad: confused (passes as human but isn't a synth)
- Brotherhood: sees her as dangerous pre-war tech
- Minutemen: doesn't care as long as she helps

## Stage Flow (Revised Concept)
1. Vault 111 exit - Claude approaches with intel
2. Sanctuary quick stop - grab Codsworth, Workshop tutorial
3. Red Rocket - Dogmeat joins, threat assessment
4. Concord rescue - tactical multi-follower assault, get Preston out fast
5. Sanctuary established - Preston's group settles in
6. Pivot east - Railroad contact (Freedom Trail)
7. Railroad foothold - Tradecraft
8. Brotherhood contact - Cambridge (bounded, not joining)
9. Institute access - tunnel route (non-relay)
10. All four factions contacted, coalition work begins
